Executive Summary

YouTick is a decentralized video streaming platform that enables content creators to monetize premium video content through NFT-based access control. By leveraging blockchain technology, YouTick eliminates intermediaries, ensures true content ownership, and provides a censorship-resistant distribution channel for concerts, cinema, and exclusive events.

"Own your content. Control your audience. Keep your revenue."

The Problem

Traditional video streaming platforms suffer from fundamental issues that harm both creators and consumers:

  • Revenue Extraction: Platforms take 30-55% of creator revenue
  • No True Ownership: Content can be demonetized, removed, or censored at will
  • Piracy Vulnerability: Premium content is easily copied and redistributed
  • Geographical Restrictions: Content locked behind regional barriers
  • No Secondary Market: Viewers cannot resell access they no longer need

The YouTick Solution

YouTick transforms premium video distribution through a Web3-native architecture:

How It Works

For Creators: Upload & Monetize

  1. Connect NEAR Wallet — Sign in with your NEAR account (e.g., artist.near)
  2. Upload Video — Content is encrypted and stored on Filecoin via Lighthouse
  3. Set Terms — Define price, supply limit, and royalty percentage
  4. Mint NFTs — Create access tokens on NEAR blockchain
  5. Earn — Receive NEAR directly to your wallet for each sale + ongoing royalties

For Viewers: Purchase & Watch

  1. Browse — Discover concerts, films, and exclusive content
  2. Purchase NFT — Buy access with NEAR (sub-second confirmation)
  3. Watch — NFT ownership automatically unlocks streaming access
  4. Keep or Sell — Hold your NFT or resell on secondary markets
