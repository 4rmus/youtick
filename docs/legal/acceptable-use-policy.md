# Acceptable Use Policy

> Draft for legal review. Repository inclusion does not make this policy
> approved or effective.

Creators must not publish unlawful content, child sexual abuse material,
non-consensual sexual content, credible threats, malware, phishing material or
content they do not have the right to distribute.

Reports may be sent to **abuse@youtick.net** and should include the publication
URL or ID, the reason for the report and optional contact details. Urgent
security-sensitive reports should use the private channel named in the
repository root security policy.

YouTick may suspend sales or record a takedown on NEAR and separately request
removal from Livepeer. On-chain restriction and provider removal are distinct
operations; removal may not be immediate. Applicable legal reporting and
preservation duties take precedence.

Creators remain responsible for rights, consent, age requirements and local
law. Refund, appeal, retention and transparency procedures require separate
approved policies before production activation.
